Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about using Typing Studio.

Is Typing Studio free?

Yes. You can practise as much as you like without creating an account.

Do I need to install a Hindi keyboard?

No. When your text is in Hindi, the page maps your normal English keys to the Remington Gail layout while you type in the practice box.

What is Remington Gail?

It is a Hindi keyboard layout based on the old Hindi typewriter. Some typing tests specify it together with the Mangal font.

Why does the page use the Mangal font?

Mangal is the Unicode Hindi font named in many typing test notifications. It appears on Windows computers. On other devices a similar font is shown instead.

What is the difference between practice mode and exam mode?

Practice mode shows live green and red marks and lets you use backspace. Exam mode turns off backspace and live marks and shows your result only at the end, like a real test.

How is net speed calculated?

Net speed = (keystrokes ÷ 5 − total mistakes) ÷ minutes. Total mistakes are full mistakes plus half the half mistakes. See the scoring article for an example.

What are full and half mistakes?

A full mistake is a missing, extra or wrong word. A half mistake is a small spelling error, a capital-letter difference or a spacing error.

Will my score match my real exam?

It is a close estimate, but exam bodies set their own rules. Check your exam notification for the official method.

Is my text or data stored anywhere?

Your text stays in your browser and is not uploaded. Your past results are saved on your own device only. See the privacy policy.

How do I clear my history?

On the practice page, scroll to the Your progress section and click Clear history, then click again to confirm.

Why does the short i sign appear before the letter while I type?

In Remington style the sign for the short i is typed before the consonant, as on a typewriter. The page shows the correct order in the text preview.

Can I type Hindi and English in the same text?

Yes. Use the Keys button, or press Ctrl+Space, to switch between the Hindi and English layouts.

Does it work on a phone?

The Remington mapping needs a physical keyboard. On a phone you can still practise English, or use your phone keyboard for Hindi.

Which timers are available?

No timer, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 45 minutes, 1 hour, or a custom time from 5 to 60 minutes.

Which browsers work best?

Recent versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.
