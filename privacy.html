Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name or email through this site. The text you paste or upload is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers.

Data stored on your device

Your past typing results (speed, accuracy and mistakes) are saved in your browser using local storage so you can see your progress. This data stays on your device. You can remove it with the Clear history button or by clearing your browser data.

Advertising and cookies

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s previous visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, or visit aboutads.info to opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies.

Other services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the site is hosted on Vercel. These services may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser loads the page.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

For questions about this policy, see the Contact page.
