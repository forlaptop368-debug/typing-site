Free Hindi and English typing practice

Typing Studio is a free typing practice tool where you choose the text. Paste a passage or upload a .txt file, pick a timer and start typing. It supports Hindi with the Mangal font and the Remington Gail layout, plus English, and it scores your work the way many exam typing tests do: by words, with full and half mistakes.

How it works

  1. Add your text. Paste it, upload a .txt file, or choose one of our practice passages.
  2. Choose your settings. Pick practice mode or exam mode, a timer from 5 minutes to 1 hour, and your scoring rule.
  3. Type and review. Follow the highlighted key on the on-screen keyboard. When you finish, you get your net and gross speed, accuracy, full and half mistakes, and a coloured review of your typed text.

Why practise with your own text?

Random words build finger speed, but exams use real passages with punctuation, names and longer sentences. Practising the kind of text you will face, such as news, official letters or general essays, builds the vocabulary and rhythm you need. It also lets you practise the exact paragraphs given by your teacher or coaching class.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. There is no sign-up and no limit on practice.

Do I need a Hindi keyboard?

No. For Hindi text the page maps your English keys to the Remington Gail layout automatically.

How is my speed calculated?

Net speed = (keystrokes ÷ 5 − total mistakes) ÷ minutes, where total mistakes = full mistakes + half mistakes ÷ 2.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. Your text and results stay in your browser.

What is exam mode?

Exam mode turns off backspace and live marks and shows your result only at the end, like a real typing test.
