How to publish your changes

  1. Click Download passages.js. This updates the list on the typing page.
  2. Click Download passages.html. This updates the public Passages page. (It works when you open this page from your live site.)
  3. In your GitHub repository, upload both files and choose Commit changes. They replace the old ones.
  4. Vercel redeploys in about 30 seconds. Press Ctrl+F5 on the site to see the new matter.

Your edits are kept in this browser until you reset them, so you can close the page and come back. This page is not linked anywhere on the site and is hidden from search engines, but anyone with the address can open it. They cannot publish anything, because only you can upload to GitHub.
