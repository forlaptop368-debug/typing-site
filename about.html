About Typing Studio

Free typing practice for Hindi and English, built around your own text.

Typing Studio lets you paste any passage or upload a text file and practise typing it. It was built for people preparing for typing tests, so it supports the Mangal font with the Remington Gail layout and scores your work the way many exams do: by words, with full and half mistakes.

What you can do

  • Practise in Hindi or English, with an on-screen keyboard that highlights the next key.
  • Choose a timer from 5 minutes to 1 hour, pause, or submit early.
  • Switch between practice mode and exam mode (no backspace, no live marks).
  • See net and gross speed, accuracy, full and half mistakes, and a coloured review of your typed text.
  • Track your progress over time on your own device.

Our approach

Everything runs in your browser. We do not ask you to sign up, and the text you practise with is not sent to a server. Scores are estimates for practice and may differ slightly from your exam, so please check your exam notification for the official rules.
