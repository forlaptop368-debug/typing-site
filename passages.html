Building a habit English · 71 words

A good habit is built one day at a time. Typing is no different. Ten focused minutes every morning will do more for your speed than a long session once a week. Keep your back straight, rest your wrists lightly, and look at the text rather than the keys. When you make a mistake, do not rush to hide it. Slow down for a moment, find the pattern, and try again.

The public library English · 64 words

Libraries have always been quiet places where people gather to learn. Today they also offer computers, free internet access and classes for people of every age. A student can prepare for an examination, a worker can learn a new skill, and a retired person can read the morning newspaper, all under one roof. A library is proof that knowledge grows when it is shared.
