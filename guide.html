Hindi typing guide

How to practise with the Mangal font and the Remington Gail layout, and how exam-style scoring works.

Start practising

Getting started

  1. Paste your passage, or upload a .txt file, on the practice page.
  2. Pick a timer. Practice mode shows live feedback and lets you use backspace.
  3. Type with your normal English keyboard. For Hindi text, the page maps each key to the Remington Gail layout for you, so you do not need to install a Hindi keyboard.
  4. Follow the highlighted key on the on-screen keyboard until the layout feels natural, then hide behind exam mode.
  5. Press Submit, or let the timer finish, to see your speed, accuracy and a coloured review of your mistakes.

How vowels and half letters work

Some vowels have no key of their own and are built from two keys. Type अ then ा to get आ. Type ा then े to get ो, and ा then ै to get ौ. Shift on most consonant keys gives the half form, for example Shift+D gives क् so you can build joined letters. Shift+= gives the halant (्).

How exam-style scoring works

Many government typing tests compare words, not single letters. A full mistake is a missing, extra or wrong word. A half mistake is a small spelling slip, a capital-letter difference or a spacing error. Total mistakes are full mistakes plus half the half mistakes. A commonly used formula is:

Net speed (WPM) = (keystrokes ÷ 5 − total mistakes) ÷ minutes

Some exams ignore the first 5% of mistakes, and the rules differ from exam to exam, so always check your own notification. The scores on this site are an estimate for practice.

Practice tips

  • Accuracy first. Every mistake costs more than a little extra speed gains.
  • Practise in short daily sessions of 10 to 15 minutes rather than one long weekly session.
  • Use the review after each test to find the words you miss most, then retype those words.
  • Switch to exam mode (no backspace, no live marks) for the last week before your test.
  • Keep your eyes on the text, not the keyboard.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install a Hindi keyboard?
No. The page maps your English keys to Remington Gail while you type in the practice box.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?
No. Your text stays in your browser.

Where are my past results saved?
On your own device only. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Can I practise English too?
Yes. English text uses a standard English keyboard guide, and you can switch layouts for mixed text.
